02 / The roadmap

Six stages, in order

Each stage builds on the one before. Open a stage to see its topics and track your progress.

  1. Stage 01FoundationsMaster the mathematical fundamentals required for advanced civil engineering study.
  2. Stage 02Core Civil ConceptsMaster statics, mechanics of materials, and structural principles essential for civil engineering.
  3. Stage 03Field SpecializationChoose your specialization: structural, geotechnical, transportation, or environmental civil engineering.
  4. Stage 04Practical ApplicationApply your knowledge through real-world projects, case studies, and hands-on problem solving.
  5. Stage 05Professional SkillsDevelop communication, teamwork, and professional development skills essential for civil engineering careers.
  6. Stage 06Advanced & Career PrepMaster advanced topics, pursue certifications, and prepare for professional engineering careers.
