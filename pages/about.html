About

About LEARNcraft

LEARNcraft teaches civil engineering one stage at a time: a six-stage roadmap, short lessons, practice and an AI tutor you can ask when you get stuck. It works in English, Russian and Uzbek, in Telegram or in the browser.

The fields of civil engineering

Civil engineering splits into a few main fields. The roadmap introduces each of them before you choose one to go deeper in.

  • Structural engineering

    Beam theory, steel and concrete design, and how structural systems carry load.

  • Geotechnical engineering

    Soil mechanics, foundation design, slope stability and site investigation.

  • Transportation engineering

    Highway design, traffic analysis and pavement engineering.

  • Construction management

    Project planning, cost estimation, scheduling and running a site.

  • Environmental engineering

    Water treatment, waste management and environmental impact assessment.

  • Materials

    Material properties, testing standards and choosing materials for a project.

How the course is organised

Six stages, in order. Each one builds on the last.

  1. Stage 01FoundationsMathematics, physics and core engineering principles.
  2. Stage 02Core Civil ConceptsStatics, mechanics of materials and structural principles.
  3. Stage 03Field SpecializationStructural, geotechnical, transportation or environmental.
  4. Stage 04Practical ApplicationProjects, case studies and hands-on problem solving.
  5. Stage 05Professional SkillsCommunication, teamwork and professional development.
  6. Stage 06Advanced & Career PrepAdvanced topics, certifications and preparing for work.

Where to start

Open the roadmap and begin with Stage 01, or ask the AI tutor a question.
